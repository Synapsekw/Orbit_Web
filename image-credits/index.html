The imagery illustrates capabilities. Stock scenes and demonstration models are not presented as Orbit commissions or identifiable customers.

Orbit & customer marks

Original Orbit wordmark and symbol retained. Customer artwork: Sinohydro, UGCC, Bay Zero and Future Kid. Trademarks belong to their owners. Their inclusion records the owner-supplied customer names, without an implied endorsement of a particular service.

Aircraft & manufacturer material

Matrice 400 and Matrice 350 RTK artwork and power/security illustrations: DJI Enterprise. Elios 3 product and inspection images: Flyability. These are manufacturer references, not Orbit project photography. Public use requires an appropriate permission or publication basis.

The M400 and M350 wireframes are converted from DJI’s official M400 STEP assembly and M350 STEP assembly. Structural edges are sampled from the CAD curves, with a simplified surface and quantized line data for browser rendering. Manufacturer CAD is used here for local evaluation; permission for public redistribution remains to be confirmed. Product artwork remains available when 3D rendering is unavailable.

Interactive aircraft models

The DJI aircraft now use the locally converted manufacturer CAD above. Elios 3: A versatile indoor inspection drone by Flyability, through its official embedded viewer. The Elios 3 is styled in navy and gold wireframe using the documented viewer controls. Flyability has not enabled model downloads; no Elios CAD or geometry file is hosted by this website.

Spatial demonstration

Stadium under construction | Drone 3D Scan by Sircher, CC BY 4.0. Adaptations include mesh simplification, texture compression, presentation transforms, point sampling, surface rasterisation and static preview rendering. It depicts Austin FC Stadium during construction. Derived views are not validated survey deliverables, georeferenced measurements or an Orbit commission.

Spherical construction panorama

Interior Construction by Sergej Majboroda / Poly Haven, CC0. The original 8K tone-mapped panorama is served as 4096 × 2048 WebP; the poster is a view of the same scene. Demonstration material.

Orbi

Orbit’s supplied Halo Companion character, using its existing Three.js model and fallback artwork. When configured, Orbi answers through OpenAI and offers a visitor-reviewed enquiry handoff. The assistant has no access to employee accounts, flight controls or private client records.
