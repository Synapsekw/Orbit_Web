BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

How often should we capture the site?+

We agree a schedule around your build programme and reporting needs. Key milestones can be added alongside regular visits.

Can you start partway through a project?+

Yes. We can begin with a current-site baseline and build a consistent record from that point forward.

How do we start?+

Share the location, timing and intended use in a project enquiry. We will discuss coverage and delivery with you before a capture plan is agreed.
