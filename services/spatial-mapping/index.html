BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Which outputs are right for our project?+

That depends on the task. We work with your team to define the coverage, outputs, and intended use before capture.

Can we use the outputs for engineering measurements?+

Tell us the required accuracy and purpose first. Capture methods, control, validation, and acceptance criteria need to be agreed for that specific scope. The homepage explorer is a visual demonstration, not a measurement tool.

How do we start?+

Share the location, timing and intended use in a project enquiry. We will discuss coverage and delivery with you before a capture plan is agreed.
