BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Which assets can you inspect in Kuwait?+

Discuss your structures, roofs, external plant, power infrastructure or construction assets with us. Feasibility depends on access, operating conditions, visibility and the inspection objective. We confirm suitable coverage before committing to a capture plan.

Does aerial imagery replace an engineering inspection?+

Imagery can support your technical assessment, but a visual record is not a structural certification or a guarantee that every defect is visible. The responsible specialist defines the acceptance criteria and interpretation required.

Can you include thermal inspection?+

Tell us the target condition and asset operating state. Thermal work needs a suitable confirmed payload, capture conditions and interpretation workflow. We agree those details separately rather than assuming every aircraft configuration includes thermal sensing.

What should we include in an inspection enquiry?+

Share the asset type, approximate dimensions, Kuwait site location, required views, access restrictions, operating window and preferred reporting format. Avoid sending confidential drawings through the public chat.

How do we start?+

Share the location, timing and intended use in a project enquiry. We will discuss coverage and delivery with you before a capture plan is agreed.
