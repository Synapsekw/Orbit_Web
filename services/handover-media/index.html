BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Can we include earlier construction footage?+

Yes. If you have documented the project with us, we can bring that material into the final story. Existing footage can also be reviewed for inclusion.

How do you decide the style of the film?+

We start with the project, the intended audience, and where the film will be used, then agree the story and deliverable formats.

How do we start?+

Share the location, timing and intended use in a project enquiry. We will discuss coverage and delivery with you before a capture plan is agreed.
