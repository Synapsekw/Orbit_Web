BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Can you cover interiors and exteriors?+

We plan both aerial and ground coverage around the spaces you need to document, subject to safe access and site conditions.

Does this replace every site visit?+

It can make remote reviews more useful, but it does not replace inspections or decisions that require physical access to the site.

How do we start?+

Share the location, timing and intended use in a project enquiry. We will discuss coverage and delivery with you before a capture plan is agreed.
