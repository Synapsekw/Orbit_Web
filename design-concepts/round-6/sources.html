These are visual concepts for Orbit’s broader drone services positioning. Industry photographs illustrate applications; they are not presented as Orbit projects.

Equipment and capabilities

  • DJI Matrice 400 and DJI Matrice 350 RTK: official product artwork. Orbit’s use of these aircraft was confirmed by the owner. Visual, thermal, and LiDAR capabilities depend on payload configuration.
  • Flyability Elios 3: official product and application imagery. Visual inspection, GPS-denied navigation, collision-tolerant cage, and payload descriptions are based on Flyability’s product information. Surveying and ultrasonic thickness payloads are optional; these concepts do not claim a particular Orbit payload inventory.

Interactive 3D

  • DJI M350 with Zenmuse L2 LiDAR Scanner by johnnokomis. CC BY 4.0. Shown using Sketchfab’s standard embedded viewer. The model’s L2 payload is illustrative.
  • Elios 3: A versatile indoor inspection drone by Flyability. Official standard embedded viewer; no model files copied.

The M400 hero is a product image with perspective and scroll effects, not an independently rotatable 3D model. The dedicated M350 and Elios 3 viewers display genuine 3D geometry and require a connection to Sketchfab.
